# Limited partner pilot protocol

## Scope

- Site, dates, owner, help contact, and incident contact:
- Participants and roles:
- Supported devices/languages/workflows:
- Approved protocol versions:
- Data categories and retention date:
- Shadow-mode and assisted-use phases:

## Before enrollment

- Complete release gates through limited-pilot readiness.
- Train each participant and document competency.
- Record the applicable consent decisions and policy version.
- Test account removal, device loss, offline recovery, and red acknowledgement.
- Confirm the current workflow remains available as a fallback.

## Daily and weekly review

Daily: unacknowledged red items, failed sync, provider failure, duplicate/lost
records, access anomalies, and user support requests.

Weekly: extraction/correction metrics, task completion/time, subgroup results,
protocol deviations, complaints, incidents, and continued consent. Record
decisions and owners.

## Stop conditions

Stop affected use immediately for a suspected critical false negative,
misdirected patient data, unauthorized access, unrecoverable evidence loss,
unsafe interpretation, protocol expiry, missing responsible supervisor, or a
participant request. Preserve evidence, follow the incident plan, notify the
responsible organization, and require documented approval before resuming.

## Completion

Report enrollment and withdrawals, exposure, outcomes, deviations, harms or
near misses, subgroup metrics, usability findings, security/privacy events,
participant feedback, limitations, and the next decision. A pilot without a
recorded incident does not prove safety.
